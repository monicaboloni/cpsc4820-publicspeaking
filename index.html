Before you begin

A spoken conversation with a virtual human

In this study you will talk out loud with a virtual human on your screen. You will be given a topic, a minute to think, and then you will speak your answer. The virtual human will then ask two short follow-up questions.

What you need

A quiet room, headphones, and a working microphone. Your voice will be recorded for later analysis.

How the session works

  1. A few questions about you. About 2 minutes.
  2. A quick microphone check. About 1 minute.
  3. The conversation. About 6 minutes.
  4. Questions about the conversation. About 4 minutes.
This browser does not support speech recognition. Please use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer.

Your responses are stored with your participant ID only.
